# Chinh sach quyen rieng tu / Privacy policy - tro-ly

Cap nhat / Updated: 2026-09-28

**Tieng Viet.** tro-ly la tro ly ca nhan tu van hanh tren may chu rieng cua mot nguoi dung duy nhat (chu so huu). Ung dung chi truy cap tai khoan Google cua chinh chu so huu, sau khi chu so huu cap quyen, de dong bo Google Calendar, Google Contacts va Google Tasks voi may chu rieng cua ho. Du lieu chi duoc luu tren may chu cua chu so huu (co ma hoa), khong ban, khong chia se cho ben thu ba va khong dung cho quang cao. Chu so huu co the thu hoi quyen bat cu luc nao tai https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

**English.** tro-ly is a self-hosted personal assistant run by a single user (the owner) on their own server. It only accesses the owner's own Google account, with the owner's consent, to sync Google Calendar, Google Contacts and Google Tasks with the owner's private server. Data is stored only on the owner's server (encrypted), is never sold or shared with third parties and is not used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
