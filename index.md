# tro-ly

Tro ly ca nhan tu van hanh (self-hosted) cua mot nguoi dung duy nhat. Ung dung dong bo lich, danh ba va viec can lam giua may chu rieng cua chu so huu va tai khoan Google cua chinh ho.

Personal self-hosted assistant used by a single person to sync their own Google Calendar, Contacts and Tasks with their private server.
